Step 01

Sign up for a free GitHub account

Head to github.com and create your account. Pick a username you actually like — because username.github.io is going to be your address.

01 · a

The username becomes your URL

Use letters, numbers and single hyphens only. No leading or trailing dashes either.

01 · b

Create a new repository

On your profile, hit the green New button. One repo, public, that's all we need.

01 · c

Name it [username].github.io

That exact naming convention is what flips on GitHub Pages — your free hosting.

01 · d

Spin up a Codespace

A Codespace is a browser-based VS Code. No installs, no setup, just code.

01 · e

Head back to your GitHub page

Keep this tab open. We'll come back to it when it's time to push your finished site live.

Step 02

Build your site with an AI assistant

Sign into WorkBuddy — or any other AI coding tool (Codex, Claude Code) that you have access to. Then just have a conversation about what you want.

02

Chat until it feels right

Tell the AI who you are, what you want on the page, and the vibe. Iterate. Don't settle for the first draft — this is the fun part.

  • Be specific. "A warm minimalist portfolio for a marketing grad, with sections for about, education, projects."
  • Ask for files. Have the AI hand you a index.html (and any extras) you can download.
  • Refine visually. Share what you don't like. "The hero is too quiet." "Make it warmer."
Step 03

Deploy in one minute

Now take those files the AI gave you and push them to the repository you created in Step 1. No terminal commands, no pipelines — just drag, drop, commit.

03 · a

Open your repo

You're back on the repo page from Step 1. Time to give it some content.

03 · b

Use the "Upload files" button

It lives just above the file list. Drag your index.html (and any css, js, images) right in.

03 · c

Commit your changes

Write any commit message ("first commit" is fine) and hit Commit changes.

03 · d

Wait a minute. That's it.

Your URL: chloehku.github.io

GitHub Pages takes about a minute to publish. Then visit [your-username].github.io — the world's your audience.
